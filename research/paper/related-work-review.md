# AivoBlock milestone #110 — scoped literature and originality review

**Review date:** 22 October 2026. **Status:** targeted, source-verified comparison of selected close alternatives; **not** a systematic or exhaustive literature search, legal freedom-to-operate analysis, peer review, patentability opinion, or proof of priority. Source dates and references checked against the publisher, arXiv record, or institution-linked paper pages available on the date above.

**Question:** Does AivoBlock's v0.1.0 *demonstrated* small, deterministic integer-inference/private-testnet workflow justify claiming it invented AI mining, trustless proof of useful work, or production-ready distributed AI? **Answer: no.** It can make a narrower, testable systems-integration and reproducibility contribution.

**Screening methodology:** targeted searches of scholarly primary-source records and original publication pages for (1) AI/ML training useful-work proposals, (2) **inference**-as-work proposals, (3) security/formal PoUW alternatives, and (4) recent surveys. Records above were included for direct overlap or for defining correctness/security claim boundaries. Work status comes from the referenced venue/hosting record (publisher vs arXiv preprint vs author's working paper). This is **not** a documented all-databases PRISMA review; source selection is not exhaustive. Novelty may still be undercut by other publications or patents not listed here.

## Findings by scientific claim

1. **"First AI blockchain" or "invented AI mining": not defensible.** Coin.AI (2019), Lihu et al. (2020), other training schemes and the newer inference-specific proposals precede or coincide with this paper.
2. **"AI inference replaces cryptographic PoW" or "miners secure a public network": false for v0.1.0.** Admission is authority-configured; block finalization uses voting power in a closed committee, **not** measurable inference difficulty, sybil-resistant miner work or public validation.
3. **"Proof of AI computation": narrowly true only as an operational demonstration of deterministic **full recomputation**.** The existing network does not contain a succinct proof, zero-knowledge AI attestation, a cheaply verified proof of SGD training or a hardness-based proof of elapsed work. A signed successful output is not a stand-alone proof of having expended the claimed work.
4. **"Novel cross-language composition": cannot be guaranteed.** The integrated Rust node/Haskell settlement/Python worker architecture and the checked research evidence are factual implementation details. We make **no claim of exclusive invention or patent novelty** of any individual ingredient or their combination without further review.
5. **"A reproducible research prototype": supported within stated boundaries.** The public ZIP permits *archived data analysis* on a fresh CI runner; it does **not** show independent cross-hardware network replication. The paper makes numerical claims only for run `39740779280` and its commit `ade6ff42737d972658e9c309f9a2d8cffbfb988c`.
6. **"Performance improvement, environmental savings or efficiency over PoW": unproven.** Service-only CPU timing and request-to-observed-finality have different trust/timing boundaries; electricity, carbon, network throughput, public-mainnet scale and matched-control service performance were not measured.
